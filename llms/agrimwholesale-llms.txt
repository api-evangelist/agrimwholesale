# Agrim Wholesale

> Agrim Wholesale Private Limited (branded AGRIM) is a Gurugram, India based B2B
> e-commerce marketplace for agricultural inputs, connecting agri-input manufacturers
> and wholesale suppliers with small rural retailers across India.

Agrim operates as an end-user product — Android applications for retailers and for
sellers, plus a marketing website. As of 2026-09-13 the company publishes **no public
developer program, API reference, SDK, webhook catalog, or machine-readable API
